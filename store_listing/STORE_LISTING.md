## Fill these in yourself (both stores need them)
- **Privacy Policy URL** — required on both stores
- **Support URL** (App Store) and **support email** (Play Store)
- **Marketing URL** (optional) — e.g. https://callfororders.in
- **App Review login** (Apple) — the app uses phone OTP and there is no SMS provider yet, so give the reviewer a demo phone number + fixed code that works in production
- **Data safety (Play) / App Privacy (Apple)** — the app collects name, phone, email, delivery addresses, order history, and payment info (handled by Razorpay / Cashfree)
- **Content rating questionnaire** (Play) — Shopping app, no violence/UGC beyond product reviews
